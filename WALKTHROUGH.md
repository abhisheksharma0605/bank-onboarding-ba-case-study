4. In a copied project, correct S008 to 101. Predict 7 accepted and 5 exceptions. The evidence
   runner asserts the original baseline; test a changed fixture using run(source=corrected_rows)

through data mapping, SQL and UAT. I checked a 12-row synthetic batch and explained why six
quarantined requests and absent business acceptance prevented release.”
